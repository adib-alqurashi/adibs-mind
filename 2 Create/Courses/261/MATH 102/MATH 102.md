## Info
- 📄 [Syllabus](<Syllabus.pdf>) (opens in Obsidian)

- **Textbook:** Larson & Edwards, *Calculus: Early Transcendental Functions*, Metric Version, 7th ed. (Cengage, 2019)
- **Covers:** definite & indefinite integrals, FTC, integration techniques, hyperbolic functions, area/volume/arc length/surface area, improper integrals, sequences & series, convergence tests, power, Taylor & Maclaurin series
